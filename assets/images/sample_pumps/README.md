## Tips for Good Demo Images

1. **Good lighting** - Ensure belt and pulleys are clearly visible
2. **Multiple angles** - Front view and side view for each fault type
3. **Clear fault visibility** - The issue should be obvious to humans
4. **Real equipment** - Actual pumps look more impressive than stock photos

## Where to Get Images

- Take photos of real water pumps (best for authenticity)
- Use smartphone camera in good lighting
- Download from free stock photo sites (Unsplash, Pexels)
- Generate synthetic images with AI image generators
